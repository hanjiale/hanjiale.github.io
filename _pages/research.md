## 🌟 Research Interests
My current research lies at the intersection of Artificial Intelligence and Business, with a focus on large language models, AI agents, information retrieval and extraction, and graph learning methods for understanding dynamic business systems.

<div class="research-area-grid">
  <section class="research-area">

    <ul>

      <li>AI Agents</li>
      <li>Information Retrieval and Extraction</li>
      <li>Graph Learning</li>
    </ul>
  </section>
  <section class="research-area">
    <h3>Applied Areas</h3>
